ELM-networks: the training algorithm

Advantages:

  a simple non-iterative procedure consisting of three steps (many traditional
     training algorithms are considerably more complex)

  very fast and straight-forward training process (it is not necessary to solve the
     tasks concerning local minima, appropriate parameter initialization, overtraining,
     etc.)

  the parameters of the hidden neurons ai and bi are independent of the training
     data and themselves, too

  ELM-networks can generate the parameters of the hidden neurons already
     before presenting the training data (conventional training techniques must ,,see"
     the training data before they set the parameters of the hidden neurons

  ELM-networks can be used with any bounded piecewise continuous non-
     constant transfer function (gradient training algorithm work with smooth
     transfer functions only)
